Privacy

  • Only the active tab is checked, and only during a session.
  • TypeSafe receives the page address without query or fragment, its title, description and up to 4,000 characters of text.
  • Form fields and editable areas are skipped. PDFs are read on your device; only a text excerpt is sent.
  • No screenshots or history. Private windows and local addresses are never checked. Session data clears when you finish.
