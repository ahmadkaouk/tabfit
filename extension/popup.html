A one-time connection

Start the local gateway, then paste the connection code it prints. Your TypeSafe key stays in the gateway.

cd tabfit
npm start

Add your key to .env first. Setup instructions are in this project’s README.

One domain per line. Subdomains are included.

Only the active tab is checked during a running session. We send the page address without query parameters or fragments, its title, description, and up to 4,000 characters of page text. Form fields and editable areas are skipped. Page text may still contain personal information; exclude sites you want kept private.

PDFs are downloaded from their original site and read locally. Only a text excerpt (up to 4,000 characters from the first 10 pages) goes to TypeSafe. Scanned, password-protected or unreadable PDFs stay accessible. The reading limit is 12 MB.

No screenshots or browsing history are collected. Private windows, local addresses, and browser settings pages are excluded. Session decisions are cleared when you finish or restart the browser. Your last task and settings stay on this device.
